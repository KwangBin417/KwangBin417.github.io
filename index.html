About

Hi, I'm KwangBin. I completed my M.S. in Electrical Engineering at KAIST, where I also received my B.S.

My goal is to build general-purpose robots that can operate reliably in the real world. My recent work has focused on reliability in robot decision-making, from uncertainty quantification to safe generative planning. I am particularly interested in two questions:

  • What properties must a learned world action model satisfy for safety and stability guarantees in its latent dynamics to remain valid for the underlying physical system?
  • How can the reasoning capabilities that LLM/VLMs have demonstrated in symbolic domains be grounded in the dynamics and consequences of physical interaction for robot decision-making, enabling them to generalize across tasks and environments?
